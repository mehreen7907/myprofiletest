About

Somewhere between coursework and hobby, most of what I make is the same instinct applied twice: if a task is repetitive or fiddly, write something that does it for me. That's shown up in answer-bank PDFs for exam prep, a recipe app with an SVG thali on the screen, and title blocks edited in bulk instead of by hand.

The other half of that instinct goes into gifts — scrapbooks, memory books, a printed magazine — where the same care for detail is aimed at people instead of problems.
